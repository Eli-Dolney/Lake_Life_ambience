# Verification — Northwoods 0.5.0

## Automated checks

- JDK 21 / NeoForge 21.1.235 compilation and 23 JUnit tests. The supported
  NeoForge JUnit runtime supplies Minecraft block shapes; fixture tags are bound
  explicitly because these tests do not load a server datapack.
- Shelter tests use actual door, glass, plank, log, stone and leaf block states.
  Coverage includes closed/open doors, empty windows, deep porches, the lab's
  exact cabin dimensions, log/mixed buildings, tree canopy, caves, covered roofs,
  stone-only buildings, and guards against unloaded chunk reads.
- Mix/lifecycle policies cover smooth attenuation, distance falloff, fire grouping
  and lit/unlit eligibility, creak spacing and resets, plus the existing lake,
  wildlife, exposure, timing and cache tests.
- Fixed a no-water eligibility bug found by the live tour: water kind NONE's
  diagnostic string could be interpreted as an eligible loon, then dereference a
  missing emitter. The regression test ensures a dry forest cannot start that timer.
- Fixed ridge exposure sampling to use a heightmap sent in client chunk packets.
  The previous no-leaves heightmap is server-only in 1.21.1; leaf cover is now
  skipped locally using bounded reads. A regression test locks this requirement.
- Audio validation: 19 registered events and 51 clip references (47 bundled files
  plus four dependency references), with subtitles, source mappings, mono 44.1 kHz
  Vorbis, finite signals, no effective peak clipping, and no loop-boundary outliers.
  The three DS daytime beds are referenced through their existing dependency events
  and removed from automatic biome playback to avoid doubling. The 0.4.1 fish
  gain overrides remain intact.
- New fire and creak recordings are CC0; source pages and modification notes are
  retained. Signal analysis is not a subjective listening test.

## Sound Lab world

The world was generated by Minecraft 1.21.1 in an isolated local server workspace.
Validation reads the saved Anvil chunks and NBT, not only the generator commands:

- 512 biome chunks / 12,288 vertical sections checked across all eight areas.
- 25 fixture block checks and safe teleport landing checks passed.
- Fifteen kiosk command blocks contain the intended teleport/time/weather controls.
- Creative mode, clear midday, fixed time/weather and disabled natural spawning
  are saved; single-player cheats are enabled during packaging.
- All staged construction completed; no force-loaded chunks remain.
- A sentinel edit survived closing and reopening the world without setup running
  again. The sentinel was removed after verification.
- A dock teleport landing initially intersected the bank; the dock approach and
  generator were corrected and safe footing revalidated.
- Time controls were executed in the actual server (night returned 18000 ticks).
  Weather functions parsed and executed successfully. Physical button interaction
  remains part of the manual walkthrough.

Reports: `build/reports/audio-validation.json` and
`build/reports/sound-lab-validation.json`. Development client logs are kept in
`run_client_050_verify.log`, `run_client_050_tour.log`, and
`run_client_050_water_tour.log`.

## Live client and remaining listening checks

The development client opens the new save with Dynamic Surroundings 0.4.5 and
Architectury 13.0.11. A development-only tour exercises fixtures and logs live
shelter, gain, rain, fire and call states; it is not included in the distributed
world. Both final tours completed without client errors. Observed checks:

- Closed-door outdoor gain approximately 0.411; open doorway approximately 0.745;
  closing the door returned to the original level.
- Porch classification, exposed roof rain, suppression under covered roofs/leaves,
  tree-canopy classification, log cabins, and no creak timer in the stone house.
- Two active fire groups reduced to one on extinction and returned to two on
  relighting. Resource reload completed and the active layers recovered.
- Frozen cabins suppressed loons/frogs. Broad river-biome water and temperate ocean
  shores were eligible; the narrow channel and small-pool listening spot were not.
- Exposed ridge wind was active (approximately 0.413 strength in the final sample).

Checked observations are saved in `build/reports/live-tour-validation.json`.
Adjacent biome areas intentionally remain acoustically connected. A classification
can reflect another sampled water body near a boundary; the small pool's test
assertion is no large-lake eligibility, not a guaranteed POND diagnostic label.

The computer-use inventory does not expose Minecraft's Java window. No subjective
listening, audible-seam certification, or visual walkthrough is claimed. Follow
SOUND_LAB.md to listen through a doorway, during a loon call, beside a fire, under
rainy roofs and trees, and in cold/river/ridge areas. Pause/resume, underwater travel
and dimension-change behavior retain their guards and require a manual in-game
walkthrough in this release. Other resource packs and third-party sound mods may
change the resulting mix.

No optional Cloth Config installation is required; its integration warning and
Minecraft's existing shader warning are unrelated to Northwoods.
